Sunny privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-10.

Sunny is a private tool used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

Sunny asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created. With it, Sunny can make one calendar named "Sunny" and add, move and remove events on that calendar only. Google does not let it see or change any other calendar or any other Google data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the events Sunny makes are stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's Google account. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising. Events never have guests, so no one is emailed.

How to remove access

Remove "Sunny" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the "Sunny" calendar in Google Calendar removes every event it made.
